# Goals — Onyx WrestleFest

## The product

A browser wrestling game modeled on the arcade classic WWF WrestleFest:
side-view arcade ring, larger-than-life fighters from Leon's Onyx universe
(Big Gold, Miss Pecan, Montaba, Max, the Cat Knights and more), singles / tag /
rumble / triple threat / ladder match types, playable solo vs CPU, locally with
friends, and online with up to 6 players.

## The quality bar (Leon's standing rule)

The reference game's quality IS the bar — damn near a carbon copy of the look
and feel: real animated moves (not still photos of moves), smooth movement (no
sliding or teleporting), a believable arena, working ropes. Rich, smooth,
fully painted/shaded art. Full, real produced sound — no chiptune.

## Current milestone

**In-Ring Gameplay Overhaul** — fix the 12 issues from Leon's playtest of
2026-11-02 (full list in MEMORY.md). Priority is what happens in the ring:
animations, movement, grappling, impacts, opponent behavior. Not menus.

## Definition of done for any build

1. Muse's checks pass (netplay compatibility + smoke test).
2. Leon playtests and approves. His verdict outranks every test result.

## Longer direction

Eventually Leon wants his own engine and native apps/programs instead of
web games. Not a current task — noted so design choices don't paint us into
a web-only corner.
