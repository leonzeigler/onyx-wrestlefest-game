TURN YOUR PHONE SIDEWAYS

Onyx WrestleFest is a wide-screen game — it plays in landscape, like the arcade cabinet.

Rotate your device and the full ring appears, touch buttons on both sides.

HOW TO PLAY — every move has its own button, and the buttons match the match: TAG and APRON are tag-team controls, so they only exist in a TAG MATCH.
GAMEPLAY UPGRADE: Punches and kicks now land when the animation connects; chain accurate hits into 3-hit combos. Hold Y (gamepad left-stick click / touch BLOCK) to guard, press T just before contact to counter, and manage your blue stamina bar. Player 2: hold O to guard, [ to counter. PHASE 4: Steel Cage: hold H by the side wall to escape. Ladder: hold H beside the ladder at center ring to retrieve the title. Keep opponents away to climb. Touch players can use the CLIMB button. Every match: Move WASD / Arrows / left stick  ·  Punch Z  ·  Kick X  ·  Grab (grapple) F  ·  Whip to the ropes G  ·  Run hold SHIFT (or Punch+Kick together)  ·  Finisher E — it glows gold on touch when it's charged. On a phone the touch pad carries PUNCH · KICK · GRAB · WHIP · RUN (hold) · FINISH, and on a controller: stick/D-pad move · A punch · B kick · X grab · LT whip · LB hold to run · RB finisher · Start = start.
TAG MATCH adds: Tag V near your partner  ·  Apron lean punch Q — your partner on the apron leans over the ropes and throws it. The touch pad adds TAG · APRON, and a controller adds Y = tag · RT = apron punch. Tag matches only — in the modes below there is no partner, so those buttons are not on the pad and the tag / apron keys and buttons do nothing in the match.
SINGLES (one-on-one): the Every-match set only — no Tag, no Apron. On the fighter select, press V to switch VS CPU / 2P VERSUS. Singles 2P versus: Player 2 picks second, then fights with Arrow keys + , punch · . kick · ; grab · ' whip · ] finisher · right SHIFT run (or a second controller on the same singles set).
ROYAL RUMBLE: the Every-match set only — no Tag, no Apron. Throw rivals out over the ropes to eliminate them.
Walk into a rival (or press Grab up close) to lock up, then mash Punch to win the grapple: Grab again = slam move now · Whip or Kick = Irish whip · Finisher fires it early when they're worn down (under 30% health). Punch a downed rival to pin; Finisher over a downed rival (under 26%) ends it in style. C = insert coin — coins buy REGAIN POWER mid-match. Bodies are solid — nobody walks through anybody, and a downed rival blocks the mat: use ↑ ↓ to move in depth and walk around them.
